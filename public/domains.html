Do I need to transfer my domain to change hosting?

You can usually keep a domain with its current registrar and update its DNS settings for new hosting. Discuss your setup before making changes.

What should I have ready for a transfer?

Access to the domain owner’s email and current registrar account. Depending on the extension, the transfer may need an authorisation code or other approval.

Can I use the same domain for my email?

Yes. Your domain can be used for your website and email, with the appropriate services and DNS settings.

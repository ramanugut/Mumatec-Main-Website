Do I need to transfer my domain to change hosting?

No. You can usually keep your domain with its current registrar and update its DNS settings for your new hosting. Check your setup before making changes.

What should I have ready for a transfer?

Access to your current registrar account and the domain owner’s email. Some domain endings also require an authorisation code.

Can I use the same domain for my email?

Yes. Your domain can be used for both your website and email, with the right services and DNS settings.

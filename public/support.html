What is the difference between a domain and hosting?

Your domain is your website address, such as yourbusiness.co.za. Hosting is the space where your website files live. You normally need both to publish a website.

Can I use a domain I already own?

Yes. Keep it with your current registrar and connect it to your hosting, or ask us about transferring it.

What is included in the hosting price?

The packages include NVMe SSD storage, hosting, email accounts, cPanel, LiteSpeed and Softaculous. Domain registration and separately ordered services cost extra. Check the storage and site limits of the package you choose.

What is the difference between monthly and yearly billing?

Monthly prices are charged each month. Yearly prices show the full amount paid for a year. Switch the billing option to compare the total; the annual packages have a lower cost than twelve monthly payments.

How do I manage my services?

For help with an existing service, contact Mumatec directly. The customer portal is being prepared.

Can Mumatec build a website for my current host?

Yes. Choose a website design package and keep your host, or host with Mumatec. Setup on another host costs R300 once-off, separate from design.

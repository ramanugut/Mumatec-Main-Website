Pricing & service details

Know what you’re choosing.

A few details to check before setting up hosting, a domain or a website.

Hosting packages

Choose monthly billing or pay for a full year upfront. Domain registration and standalone email services are priced separately. Compare each package’s website and storage limits before choosing.

Domains

Domain prices depend on the domain ending. Availability, transfer requirements and renewal fees are confirmed before registration.

Website design

Hosting, domain registration and ongoing maintenance are separate from the listed website design fees. Mumatec confirms the pages, content and scope before work begins.

Hosting packages

Choose monthly billing or pay for a full year upfront. Domain registration and standalone email services are priced separately. Compare each package’s website and storage limits before choosing.

Domains

New domain registrations are offered with Mumatec hosting. Hosting requires a new domain or one you already own. Domain prices depend on the ending; availability and renewal fees are confirmed before registration.

Website design

Website design can use Mumatec hosting or your own host. Setup on another host costs R300 once-off. Hosting, domain registration and ongoing maintenance are separate from the listed website design fees. Mumatec confirms the pages, content and scope before work begins.
